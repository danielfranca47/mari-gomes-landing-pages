Questions

Everything you need
to know before booking

Is this a sexual or escort service? +
No. All sessions are strictly therapeutic — bodywork and energy work focused on releasing tension and restoring wellbeing. The studio is professional, private, and respectful at all times.
Which massage should I choose? +
It depends on what you're looking for: Holistic Energy Massage works with physical tension and energy together, Relaxation & Stress Relief is more grounding and calming, and Couples Massage is a shared session for two. Not sure? Send a message on WhatsApp and Mari will help you decide.
How does pricing work? Can I get a quote? +
Pricing depends on the therapy and duration you choose. Send a message via WhatsApp for a personalised quote with all the details.
Where do sessions take place? +
All sessions take place in Mari's private studio in Amsterdam. The address and exact directions are shared upon booking confirmation.
What should I wear to a session? +
Wear comfortable, easy-to-remove clothing such as loose trousers and a simple top. Please arrive already showered if possible — a shower is available on-site as well.
Are there any contraindications? +
Sessions are not recommended for those with fever, deep vein thrombosis, fractures, severe osteoporosis, open wounds, or high-risk pregnancy. If you have any health concerns, please mention them when booking.
