Discover the Art of Conscious Touch
This intimate Tantrana workshop is an invitation to slow down, become more present and discover how touch can create deeper connection.
Throughout the day, you will learn the foundations of Tantra massage through a combination of guided teaching, meditation, music meditation ,ritual, conscious touch and practical experience.
The workshop is intentionally limited to only 12 participants, allowing to create a personal atmosphere and give individual guidance during the practices.
You don’t need previous experience with Tantra or massage. But if you are experienced , you will still learn a lot.
You simply need curiosity, openness and a willingness to explore conscious touch.
Couples & Singles Are Welcome
You can join the workshop with your partner or come on your own.
If you are single, you can still fully participate in the workshop.
We can potentilly pair you with another suitable participant or, where possible, arrange someone from the trusted circle for you to learn and practice with.
The workshop is designed to create a respectful and comfortable environment for everyone.
A Small & Personal Workshop
The workshop is limited to 12 participants / 6 couples.
Keeping the group small allows me to be personally involved in the practices and provide individual guidance.
This is not a large group seminar where you simply watch from a distance. You will have the opportunity to learn, practice, ask questions and receive guidance throughout the day.
THE MORNING SESSION
Conscious Touch & Tantra Massage for Women
Welcome & Opening Ritual
We begin by arriving in the space and getting to know one another. 
Through music, meditation and a Tantric transfiguration practice, we create a relaxed and connected atmosphere before moving into the educational part of the workshop.
The Art of Conscious Touch
Touch is much more than technique.
You will learn how to become more present with your hands, how to slow down, how to vary pressure and movement, and how to become more attentive to your partner’s responses.
We explore the difference between simply touching the body and truly listening through touch.
Tantra Massage for Women
The morning lecture introduces the foundations of Tantra massage for women. 
We discuss basic anatomy, sensitivity, different areas of the body and how individual women can experience touch differently. 
The focus is on awareness, communication, presence and learning to respond to the person in front of you rather than simply following a fixed technique.
Guided Practice
After the teaching, you will put your new knowledge into practice. 
Women receive a guided Tantra massage from their partners while I move through the space, offering instruction, support and individual guidance. 
You don’t need to remember everything perfectly. I will guide you step by step.
THE EVENING SESSION
Conscious Touch & Tantra Massage for Men
Tantra Massage for Men
We continue with a lecture focused on Tantra massage for men.
We explore male anatomy, sensitivity, conscious touch, communication and the foundations of a Tantric approach to massage, including an introduction to lingam massage. 
The emphasis is on presence and connection rather than simply learning a sequence of techniques.
Guided Practice
The men then receive a guided Tantra massage from their female partners. 
Me and my colleague remain present throughout the practice, offering guidance and helping you understand how to listen to your partner’s body and responses.
Practical Information
What you will take away
By the end of the workshop, you will have explored:
• The foundations of Tantra massage
• Conscious and present touch
• Different qualities of touch and pressure
• Basic body awareness and anatomy
• Communication through touch
• How to listen to your partner’s responses
• Giving and receiving Tantra massage
• A deeper awareness of your own body and your partner’s body • Yoni and lingam massage
• Erotogenic zones on partner body and how to detect them
More than learning techniques, the intention is to help you discover a different quality of connection through touch.
