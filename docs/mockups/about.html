The story behind the practice

My path into this work took years of curiosity, training, and practice — learning to work with the body not just as muscle and tissue, but as something that holds tension, emotion, and memory. That path is what shaped the practice I now offer here in Amsterdam.

Holistic and tantric massage isn't about a fixed sequence of moves. It works with breath, presence, and unhurried touch to help the body release what it's been holding — so energy can move again, and you can feel more grounded, more spacious, and more like yourself.

My approach blends holistic bodywork, energy work, and tantric practice — shaped session by session around what you need, not a fixed formula. Every session starts with a short conversation, so the touch that follows actually meets you where you are that day.

What guides my work isn't a script — it's presence. I trained to read the body, not just move through a routine, and I bring that attention into every session: pacing, pressure, and rhythm all respond to you in real time.

Outside the studio, I care about creating a space where people feel safe enough to actually receive — no performance, no rush, just permission to be in your body again.

— Mari
