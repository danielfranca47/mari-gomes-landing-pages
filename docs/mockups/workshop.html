Tantra Massage Workshop

A Day of Conscious Touch, Connection & Tantra

Discover the art of conscious touch in an intimate day for couples and singles in Amsterdam.

This intimate workshop is an invitation to slow down, become more present, and discover how touch can create deeper connection. Throughout the day, you will learn the foundations of Tantra massage through a combination of guided teaching, meditation, music meditation, ritual, conscious touch, and practical experience.

You don't need previous experience with Tantra or massage — but if you are experienced, you will still learn a lot. You simply need curiosity, openness, and a willingness to explore conscious touch. The group is kept small so I can be personally involved in the practices and give everyone individual guidance.

Program

What Happens on the Day

Morning Session — Conscious Touch & Tantra Massage for Women

Welcome & Opening Ritual. We begin by arriving in the space and getting to know one another. Through music, meditation, and a Tantric transfiguration practice, we create a relaxed and connected atmosphere before moving into the educational part of the workshop.

The Art of Conscious Touch. Touch is much more than technique. You will learn how to become more present with your hands, how to slow down, how to vary pressure and movement, and how to become more attentive to your partner's responses — the difference between simply touching the body and truly listening through touch.

Tantra Massage for Women. The morning lecture introduces the foundations of Tantra massage for women — basic anatomy, sensitivity, and how individual women can experience touch differently — with a focus on awareness, communication, presence, and responding to the person in front of you rather than following a fixed technique.

Guided Practice. Women receive a guided Tantra massage from their partners while I move through the space, offering instruction, support, and individual guidance. You don't need to remember everything perfectly — I will guide you step by step.

Evening Session — Conscious Touch & Tantra Massage for Men

Tantra Massage for Men. We continue with a lecture focused on Tantra massage for men — male anatomy, sensitivity, conscious touch, communication, and the foundations of a Tantric approach to massage, including an introduction to lingam massage. The emphasis is on presence and connection rather than simply learning a sequence of techniques.

Guided Practice. The men then receive a guided Tantra massage from their female partners. I remain present throughout the practice, offering guidance and helping you understand how to listen to your partner's body and responses.
