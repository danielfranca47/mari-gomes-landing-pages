Worldwide, subject to availability and advance planning — message me with your destination and dates and I'll confirm.

Simply message me with your city, preferred dates, number of participants, and the type of session you wish to experience. I will respond with a personalized proposal. If it's easier to contact me via WhatsApp first, that's fine too.

Booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance is recommended to secure your preferred dates and allow time for planning a tailored experience.

Absolutely. I lead group workshops, Tantra Massage trainings, and private retreats, customized for your group's size and goals.

Each session is designed around your intentions, comfort level, and desired outcome. Whether it's healing, learning, or deepening intimacy, every experience is unique, safe, and transformative.
What are the pricing options? +
Pricing depends on session type, duration, group size, and travel logistics. After your inquiry, I provide a detailed proposal including all costs.
Are sessions confidential and private? +
Yes. Every experience is conducted with complete discretion, ensuring a safe and private environment for your journey.
What if my preferred dates are not available? +
I will work with you to find alternative dates or help plan a future Fly Me In experience.
